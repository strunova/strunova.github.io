Release notes

What’s new in STRUNOVA

Every alpha version, newest first. Each one is offered in the download section as soon as it is ready.

0.21.0 Latest

2026-11-18

Guitars of 7 and 8 strings

  • Choose your guitar in Settings: six, seven or eight strings, in standard tuning, with a low B on the seventh string and a low F♯ on the eighth.
  • The positions of every chord, the whole neck, chord identification, progressions, favorites and the sound follow the guitar you choose.
  • On seven and eight strings, the positions of the library are played as written, with the extra low strings not played, and marked as six-string positions.

0.20.1

2026-11-17

Lighter animations, and fixes

  • The animations ask much less of a laptop: the glass keeps its look without blurring what lies behind it.
  • Playing every position of a chord in turn no longer stalls when you click something else meanwhile.
  • Settings that cannot be read are never overwritten, and a language that cannot be saved stays as it was.
  • The light of a pressed button comes from the middle when you press it with the keyboard.

0.20.0

2026-11-16

Glass, in the manner of iOS 26 and macOS Tahoe

  • The sidebar floats in the window as a pane of glass that lets the light through.
  • Buttons, fields, tabs and choices are capsules of glass with a rim that catches the light; the main action is gold glass. Tables, diagrams and texts stay solid, easy to read.
  • A selection stretches like a drop as it slides, glass lights up where you press it, and a slider’s handle or a switch’s knob turns into a lens of clear glass while you hold it.

0.19.0

2026-11-16

More chords

  • Six more chord types: 7♯5, 7♭5, 9sus4, maj7♯11, m11 and m6/9, each with an explanation of its theory.
  • The m6 and m(maj7) chords on every root, and more positions for maj7: 359 chords with 609 positions.
  • A review of every chord in the library: the fingering of the open Gmaj7 is corrected.

0.18.0

2026-11-15

English

  • The interface in English as well as Romanian: choose the language in Settings; until you do, STRUNOVA follows the language of your system.
  • Every text in English: the sections, the names and explanations of the chords, and Lesson 1.
  • Labels that were cut off at the edge of four lesson drawings, and long names in the whole sound path, now fit.

0.17.0

2026-11-15

The Grif section

  • Every position of a chord from the library on the whole neck, each one marked above it.
  • The chosen position drawn in full on the neck, with fingers, notes or degrees, and large under it, to hear and to keep among your favorites.
  • Choose a position with a click on its mark or on one of its faint notes, with the arrows or with the keyboard.

0.16.0

2026-11-15

The theory of every chord

  • The card of a chord has two views, Positions and Theory.
  • Theory shows the formula, each note with its interval above the root and its distance in semitones, the bass and the inversion.
  • An explanation of each of the 25 chord types, written from open music theory textbooks, with its sources.
  • The table of chords no longer cuts off long names in narrower windows.

0.15.1

2026-11-15

Fixes

  • The search finds the 6/9 chord written as musicians write it, such as C6/9.
  • In chord identification, the bass label goes on the lowest note, not on the lowest string played.
  • Typing a chord in the search never makes it sound.
  • The answers of the test are kept during a visit to another section, and no question can be skipped.
  • Settings changed in the first moments after the app opens no longer replace the saved ones.

0.15.0

2026-11-14

Lesson 1 is complete

  • Chapters on the amplifier and on effects pedals, with their drawings.
  • The whole sound path in an interactive diagram, for each kind of guitar.
  • A test of ten questions at the end of the lesson, with the score.

0.14.5

2026-11-14

More drawings in Lesson 1

  • How a guitar can play its effects through its own body, and how that differs from the pickup of a classic electric guitar.
  • Twelve new drawings, among them guitars drawn in the style of a classic electric guitar and of a smart guitar.

0.14.0

2026-11-14

More chapters of Lesson 1

  • The acoustic and electro-acoustic guitar: steel strings, pickups, the preamplifier, feedback.
  • Guitars that play their effects through their own body.
  • The electric guitar: magnetic pickups, single-coil and humbucker, volume and tone.

0.13.0

2026-11-14

Lessons

  • The sidebar shows the STRUNOVA icon.

Favorites

Settings
