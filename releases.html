What’s new in STRUNOVA

Every alpha version, newest first. Each one is offered in the download section as soon as it is ready.

0.15.0 Latest

2026-11-14

Lesson 1 is complete

  • Chapters on the amplifier and on effects pedals, with their drawings.
  • The whole sound path in an interactive diagram, for each kind of guitar.
  • A test of ten questions at the end of the lesson, with the score.

0.14.5

2026-11-14

More drawings in Lesson 1

  • How a guitar can play its effects through its own body, and how that differs from the pickup of a classic electric guitar.
  • Twelve new drawings, among them guitars drawn in the style of a classic electric guitar and of a smart guitar.

0.14.0

2026-11-14

More chapters of Lesson 1

  • The acoustic and electro-acoustic guitar: steel strings, pickups, the preamplifier, feedback.
  • Guitars that play their effects through their own body.
  • The electric guitar: magnetic pickups, single-coil and humbucker, volume and tone.

0.13.0

2026-11-14

Lessons

  • The Theory section starts with Lesson 1, about the guitar and its sound: what sound is, the parts of a guitar, the sound path of a classical guitar.
  • Lessons read card by card, with drawings and notes to hear; the chapters read are remembered.
  • The sidebar shows the STRUNOVA icon.

0.12.0

2026-11-12

Favorites

  • A star on every chord keeps it with the position shown.
  • The Favorites section shows the starred chords as cards to see, hear and open in the library.

0.11.0

2026-11-11

Settings

  • The volume is kept from one session to the next.
  • Sound as soon as a note or a chord is chosen, a fretboard of 12, 15 or 17 frets, and note names under the mouse.
  • Settings are saved automatically, on your computer only.

0.10.5

2026-11-11

Motion in the manner of iOS and macOS

  • Buttons give way under a click and spring back; selections slide.
  • Sliders swell while held and glide to a click; fingers glide to the next position.

0.10.0

2026-11-10

A new design

  • Warm, dark surfaces and a gold accent, new fonts and icons.
  • The chord library as a table of chord types and roots, with the chosen chord beside it.
  • A wooden fretboard for chord identification, with an Example button, and example progressions to try.
  • The voicing with open strings now comes first among voicings at the same place on the neck.

0.9.0

2026-11-10

Filters for the positions of a chord

  • Filter by level, open or barre, place on the neck and number of fingers.
