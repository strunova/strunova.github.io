Roadmap

Where STRUNOVA is going

STRUNOVA is built in phases, and each one is tested before the next begins. Here is what is done, what is being built now and what comes next.

Updated on 2026-11-15. Every finished version is in What’s new.

  1. Foundation Done

    The STRUNOVA window and its sections, the icon, a strict security setup, and automatic checks that test the app at every change.

  2. A chord library you can trust Done

    Documented chords, every position checked by the app’s music engine. Search by name, with other names and enharmonic spellings, and a clear message when a chord is not in the library: nothing is made up.

  3. Chord diagrams Done

    A clear diagram for every position: fingers, barres, open and muted strings, the root and the notes.

  4. Sound Done

    Plucked strings synthesized in the app, in tune to within a cent: hear every position strummed or string by string.

  5. Advanced features In progress

    The tools around the library, one version at a time.

    Done

    • Chord identification: choose notes on a fretboard and find their chord.
    • A library of 269 chords and 459 positions, from power chords to altered ninths.
    • Build music: write a progression, then hear it in time.
    • Filters for the positions of a chord.
    • A new design, with motion in the manner of iOS and macOS.
    • Settings and favorite chords, kept on your computer.
    • Lesson 1: the guitar and its sound.
    • The theory of every chord: formula, intervals, bass and an explanation.
    • The Grif section: every position of a chord on the whole neck.
    • The interface in English as well as Romanian, chosen in Settings.

    Next

    • More chords and positions in the library.
    • Guitars with 7 and 8 strings.
    • Every position of the library played on a guitar, not only checked.

  6. The website Done

    This website: the features with real screenshots, the download with its checksum, the release notes and this roadmap.

  7. Testing and launch Planned

    The road from the alpha to the first full version.

    • Testing the whole app: its musical data, its speed and its security.
    • Easier updates, without downloading every version by hand.
    • More lessons.
    • Versions for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows, on this website.
    • The final documentation, and the launch.
